# Release Vision – MVP 1

**Audience:** Adults with ADHD who overspend or miss bills. (One audience per MVP. Other audiences go to later MVPs.)

## Vision
Stop paying the ADHD tax. We help adults with ADHD never miss a bill, catch impulse buys before they happen, and stop paying for forgotten subscriptions — automatically, without shame.

## Problems
- They forget bills → late fees and overdrafts
- Impulse buys, then regret
- Forgotten subscriptions
- (Business) churn
- (Business) trust with bank data

## Goals
- Never pay a late fee; less to remember; trust the app to remember
- Buy on purpose; keep money for what matters; no shame
- Know every subscription; stop paying for unused ones; money back each month
- Trust the app with bank data

## Use cases
- Link bank → see a safe-to-spend number
- Recurring bills found automatically
- Reminder before and on the due day; one-tap "paid" / "remind me later"
- Upcoming bills calendar
- Pause-before-purchase check against safe-to-spend
- Wishlist with a waiting period
- Alert when near the limit
- Kind wording
- Subscriptions found automatically
- Trial and renewal warnings
- Monthly subscription total
- Simple cancel steps
- Sign up, log in, delete data

## Later MVPs (not in MVP 1)
- MVP 2: newly / late-diagnosed (self-blame, time blindness timeline)
- MVP 3: self-identified neurodivergent / AuDHD (overwhelm, sensory and notification controls)
- Budget-app dropouts (quick start, welcome back)
