# Scope of Outcomes – MVP 1

Audience: Adults with ADHD who overspend or miss bills.

## 1. The app remembers your bills for you
**Problem:** They forget bills (busy weeks, yearly/irregular bills, hyperfocus, buried emails), so they pay late fees and overdraw their account.

Outcomes:
- Adults with ADHD can see every recurring bill without having to list them from memory.
- Adults with ADHD get reminded before a bill is due and on the due day.
- Adults with ADHD can mark a bill paid, or snooze it, in one tap.
- Adults with ADHD can see which bills are coming up before payday.
- Adults with ADHD are no longer surprised by yearly or irregular bills.
- Adults with ADHD pay fewer late fees and overdraft fees.

## 2. A pause at the moment of decision
**Problem:** They buy on impulse (late at night, when bored or stressed, during sales, right after payday) and regret it later; apps only report spending afterward.

Outcomes:
- Adults with ADHD can check what a purchase does to today's safe-to-spend number before they buy.
- Adults with ADHD can park a want on a wishlist instead of buying it right away.
- Adults with ADHD get a heads-up when they are close to their limit.
- Adults with ADHD make fewer purchases they regret later.
- Adults with ADHD keep more money for the things that matter to them.
- Adults with ADHD decide to buy on purpose, not on autopilot.

## 3. Every subscription in one place, before it charges
**Problem:** They forget subscriptions (free trials turn paid, yearly renewals, small charges blend together) and keep paying for things they don't use.

Outcomes:
- Adults with ADHD can see every subscription they pay for in one list.
- Adults with ADHD know how much their subscriptions cost in total each month.
- Adults with ADHD get warned before a free trial turns into a paid plan.
- Adults with ADHD get warned before a yearly renewal charges.
- Adults with ADHD know the simple steps to cancel what they don't use.
- Adults with ADHD get money back each month from cancelled subscriptions.

## 4. Calm, not shame
**Problem:** Money brings anxiety and shame; red numbers and "you overspent" alerts make them avoid their money until there's a surprise.

Outcomes:
- Adults with ADHD can open the app without feeling judged.
- Adults with ADHD see kind, non-judging words, even after a bad week.
- Adults with ADHD look at their money more often instead of avoiding it.
- Adults with ADHD feel in control instead of reacting to surprises.
- Adults with ADHD trust the app to remember for them, so there's less to hold in their head.
- Adults with ADHD feel less anxious about money over time.
